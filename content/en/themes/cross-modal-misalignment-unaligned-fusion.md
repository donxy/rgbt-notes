### Scope and Definitions

This theme includes all core papers carrying the alignment tag (source: the alignment tag in papers.csv, 13 papers). The object of treatment is the cross-modality spatial inconsistency that remains after registration: from weak misalignment of a few pixels to the large geometric differences produced by composite affine transformations. The spectrum of methods is divided by mechanism into four categories: explicit offset regression alignment, implicit attention calibration, explicit registration brought back into the pipeline, and reliability assessment and contextual compensation after alignment; the mechanism of each member is as recorded in its fusion_strategy field.

### Overview within the Library

- Explicit offset regression is the earliest category to take shape: translation, and even translation–scale–rotation deviations, are regressed at the region (RoI/proposal) level, and region features are aligned before fusion (examples: zhang2019weakly, yuan2022translation, zhang2025weakly) (source: the fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- The implicit calibration route does not model the offset itself, but instead lets attention or deformable sampling search for the corresponding position in the other modality to complete aggregation (examples: yuan2024c2former, fu2024cfdeformable, song2024misaligned) (source: the fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- For large misalignment, explicit registration is brought back into the pipeline: cross-modality affine registration transforms the features of one modality level by level into the coordinate system of the other modality before fusion, and the object of treatment is composite affine misalignment on the order of tens to hundreds of pixels (example: ming2026bridging) (source: the problem and robustness_detail of ming2026bridging).
- Asking, after alignment, "whether the alignment result can be trusted" is a newer branch: a reliability map is constructed from the consistency of features after alignment to guide fusion, or global semantic context is used to compensate for the local feature degradation at misaligned locations (examples: hou2026leryolo, jin2025contextuallyguided) (source: the fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- Coverage of controlled misalignment evaluation is uneven: among the 13 papers, 5 have a controlled misalignment protocol with a given offset (single-stream translation, grid, or tiered scanning), 2 are at abstract level and are judged Unknown, and the remaining members either have only the natural misalignment inherent in the dataset itself, or misalignment is a task setting in which training and testing share the same distribution rather than an evaluation protocol (examples: fu2024cfdeformable, chen2024weakly, song2024misaligned) (source: statistics over the robustness_evaluation and robustness_detail fields of the members, Yes 6/13, of which 5 are controlled misalignment, Unknown 2/13).
- In many works, the reference modality for alignment is fixed to one side (in most cases thermal infrared), and when the reference side is unreliable there is no mechanism for switching or downweighting; this point recurs in the limitations of the respective papers (examples: zhang2019weakly, zhang2025weakly, chen2024weakly) (source: the Limitations of each of the above papers).
- The magnitude of misalignment faced by the members differs greatly: in small-target scenarios, a displacement of a few pixels can already break cross-modality correspondence, the residual offsets in pre-registered datasets are mostly in the range 0–15 pixels, whereas severe misalignment settings reach tens to hundreds of pixels; the three cannot be compared directly with one another (examples: hou2026leryolo, chen2024weakly, ming2026bridging) (source: the problem and robustness_detail of each of the above papers).

### Representative Works

- zhang2019weakly: uses RFA to regress offsets proposal by proposal to align region features, followed by confidence-weighted fusion, and provides a controlled misalignment evaluation with 169 grid offsets and four-direction ±10 px scanning (source: the fusion_strategy and robustness_detail of zhang2019weakly).
- yuan2022translation: explicitly regresses the three coupled geometric deviations of translation, scale, and rotation at the RoI level, followed by element-wise addition fusion, for aerial RGB-IR image pairs (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of yuan2022translation).
- fu2024cfdeformable: an alignment-free route — cross-modality deformable attention searches the infrared features for corresponding key points for sampling and aggregation, with evaluation by six-tier scanning with fixed offsets (source: the fusion_strategy and robustness_detail of fu2024cfdeformable).
- song2024misaligned: provides a UAV visible–thermal benchmark with native misalignment (DVTOD), in which the baseline detector completes feature-level alignment internally through attention correction, global affine alignment, and channel-wise correction (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of song2024misaligned).
- hou2026leryolo: first performs uncertainty-aware alignment, and then uses a reliability map to guide sparse expert routing fusion, incorporating whether the local correspondence after alignment is trustworthy into the fusion decision (source: the fusion_strategy of hou2026leryolo).
- ming2026bridging: for severe misalignment, performs cross-modality affine registration level by level back in the feature domain and conducts end-to-end joint detection after alignment, with misalignment injected by composite affine transformations (rotation, scaling, translation) (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of ming2026bridging).

### Open Problems (As Stated in the Papers)

- The benchmark provides misaligned data, but does not provide a measure of misalignment: misalignment is neither quantified nor graded, so later work cannot make stratified comparisons by degree of misalignment (source: the Limitations of song2024misaligned).
- The misalignment in the evaluation is entirely artificial rigid translation applied to strictly aligned original images, and natural misalignment is not tested; moreover, there is no single-modality control row, so it cannot be judged whether, under offset, fusion remains superior to discarding one modality (source: the Limitations of fu2024cfdeformable).
- Offset estimation is unsupervised and has no evaluation of alignment error, so the correctness of alignment is not falsifiable; the infrared reference modality is trusted unconditionally (source: the Limitations of chen2024weakly).
- The magnitude of implicit calibration has never been measured: there is no controlled experiment with synthetic offsets, and fine offsets smaller than the sampling stride are invisible to attention; attention has no concept of quality and cannot abstain, so when the content of one modality is damaged, the contamination path remains fully open (source: the Limitations of yuan2024c2former).
- Alignment is asymmetric and the reference is fixed, with no mechanism for switching or downweighting when the reference side is unreliable (source: the Limitations of zhang2025weakly).
- The global rigidity assumption of a single image-level affine transformation cannot express parallax and non-rigid deformation, and registration accuracy itself has never been evaluated directly (source: the Limitations of ming2026bridging).
