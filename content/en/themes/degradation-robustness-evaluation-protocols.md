### Scope and Definition

This theme collects all papers judged as Yes for robustness_evaluation in the comparison table, that is, those for which a controlled degradation evaluation exists in the public deep-reading records: at least one of corruption injection, whole-modality missing/zeroing, controlled misalignment, or graded severity scanning (source: compare_fields.csv, statistics for the robustness_evaluation column, 31/102). The objects of evaluation are not limited to RGB–thermal detection: of the 31 papers, 15 are core and 16 are appendix papers, the latter including adjacent tasks such as LiDAR–camera 3D detection, multimodal classification and sentiment analysis, medical segmentation, and single-modality test-time adaptation (source: statistics for the list field of the members of this theme, 15/31 core, 16/31 appendix). The protocol details of each member are governed by its robustness_detail field; this section only provides a typology and does not compare numerical values across protocols.

### Landscape in the Library

- Whole-modality missing/dropping is one of the most frequent forms of degradation, constructed in various ways: copying the surviving modality to simulate loss, whole-modality zeroing (blackout), and scanning by missing rate or by enumeration of modality subsets all occur (examples: xiao2024gmdetr, kim2021mlpd, ma2021smil) (source: the robustness_detail of each of the above papers).
- Digital corruption suites constitute another main line: -C-style benchmarks organise evaluation as a matrix of "corruption type × severity level", ranging in scale from 14 corruptions for single-modality infrared to 27 types × 5 levels for 3D detection (examples: medeiros2026wiseod, beemelmanns2024multicorrupt, dong2023benchmarking) (source: the robustness_detail of each of the above papers).
- Controlled geometric misalignment is treated by several RGB-T works as an independent degradation axis for evaluation, in a protocol form that applies a given pixel offset to a single pathway and scans over levels (examples: zhang2019weakly, fu2024cfdeformable, hou2026leryolo) (source: the robustness_detail of each of the above papers).
- The practice of evaluating "damaged" and "missing" separately is found in only a few members: their protocols report partial damage such as whole-pathway dropping, beam-count reduction, field-of-view restriction, and occlusion in separate groups from whole-modality unavailability (examples: park2025resilient, essl2026sbbevfusion, cha2024robust) (source: the robustness_detail of each of the above papers).
- Degradation is applied mainly to a single pathway: the defining protocol of some members is precisely "exactly one modality is damaged, the rest remain clean"; only a minority incorporate simultaneous damage to both modalities, missing modalities, and mixed offsets into the same evaluation framework (examples: yang2024testtime, guo2025smoothing, wu2026bridging) (source: the robustness_detail of each of the above papers).
- Compound corruption and graded scanning are not standard: in the main text, some members test only a single intensity for each degradation, and graded scanning appears only in supplementary material or does not exist (examples: tian2025learning, wu2026bridging, xue2023dynamic) (source: the robustness_detail of each of the above papers).
- Real sensor faults are essentially absent from the library: among the 31 papers with robustness_evaluation=Yes, the robustness_detail of 27 papers notes that real sensor faults (such as stripe noise, NUC drift, dead pixels, and thermal saturation) were not evaluated, and all other evaluations are synthetic corruption, synthetic failure, or feature-level noise injection (examples: tian2025learning, medeiros2026wiseod, han2021trusted) (source: compare_fields.csv, statistics for the robustness_detail column, 27/31).

### Representative Work

- dong2023benchmarking: Constructs KITTI-C / nuScenes-C / Waymo-C, and uniformly evaluates 3D detectors with 27 types of synthetic corruption × 5 severity levels, including two types of alignment corruption, spatial misalignment and temporal misalignment (source: the robustness_detail of dong2023benchmarking).
- beemelmanns2024multicorrupt: An open-source benchmark for LiDAR–camera fusion robustness, with 10 types of synthetic corruption × 3 levels, including spatiotemporal misalignment of both modalities, and the relative retention rate RA as the metric (source: the robustness_detail of beemelmanns2024multicorrupt).
- wu2026bridging: An example with relatively wide coverage in RGB-T detection — five types of degradation, namely Gaussian blur, low light, additive noise, modality dropping, and misalignment; the supplementary material additionally provides a three-level intensity scan, five types of compound corruption, and whole-pathway zeroing, with the retention rate as the metric (source: the robustness_detail of wu2026bridging).
- park2025resilient: LiDAR–camera 3D detection, in which missing and partial damage are evaluated separately and are mostly applied to a single pathway, and graded scanning is performed for beam count, field of view, and the proportion of points deleted within boxes (source: the robustness_detail of park2025resilient).
- guo2025smoothing: An evaluation of multimodal test-time adaptation, typed by weak OOD (single-modality corruption) and strong OOD (simultaneous corruption of both modalities, whole-modality missing, and mixtures of missing and corruption), with scans over mixture proportions and severity (source: the robustness_detail of guo2025smoothing).
- medeiros2026wiseod: One side of a corruption benchmark for single-modality infrared transfer — applies 14 types of ImageNet-C/COCO-C-style digital corruption to infrared test sets to construct LLVIP-C / FLIR-C, and provides severity curves (source: the robustness_detail of medeiros2026wiseod).

### Open Questions (As Stated by the Papers Themselves)

- Whole-pathway loss is the only failure form, and it is simulated by copying the surviving modality rather than by zeroing or real sensor failure; in the same evaluation, when RGB is lost, the fusion result is still below the pure RGB single-modality baseline, and the original text provides no positive comparison showing that fusion is no worse than a single modality (source: the Limitations of xiao2024gmdetr).
- The benchmark has only the relative retention rate RA, with no single-modality or late-fusion control, so "whether fusion is worse than discarding one pathway" is not measured within this protocol; the corruptions are all synthetic and from the single data source nuScenes (source: the Limitations of beemelmanns2024multicorrupt).
- Missing-modality handling relies on an externally given oracle availability flag, and there is neither a mechanism nor an evaluation for the case of "present but silently degraded" (source: the Limitations of essl2026sbbevfusion).
- Routing supervision consists of identity labels for modality dropping rather than quality estimation; no continuous degradation samples are seen during training, and there is no training signal for a modality that is present but contaminated and confidently wrong; failure modelling is centred on information removal, with no real sensor fault model (source: the Limitations of park2025resilient).
- The evaluation protocol covers only the setting of "exactly one modality damaged"; simultaneous damage to both modalities, modality missing, and mixed offsets are all uncovered (source: the Limitations of yang2024testtime).
- Controlled degradation uses only the family of global affine transformations; the quality mask is estimated from the degraded features themselves (self-referential), its correlation with the true degree of degradation has not been verified, and robustness is tested at only a single-point intensity, with no severity curve (source: the Limitations of tian2025learning).
- The generation methods, intensity parameters, and subset sample sizes for the three types of degradation — overexposure, underexposure, and blur — are all unreported in the original text, so cross-degradation conclusions cannot be reproduced exactly from the original text (source: the Limitations of hao2026distributionaligned).
