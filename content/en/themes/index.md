This page organizes the 102 works included in this library by research theme (core 74 / appendix 28). Each theme provides a scope definition, an overview within the library, representative works, and open problems. **All research judgments retain their sources**: counts come from field statistics in papers.csv and compare_fields.csv (with the column name and the counting basis indicated); the "Open Problems" include only the limitations stated in the close-reading record of each paper, attributed item by item to the specific paper, and no field-level assertions without a source are made. For the judgment criteria (the definitions of the four columns of quality estimation, suppression, compensation, and robustness evaluation), see the compare field judgment standards; the data on this page are synchronized with papers.csv / compare_fields.csv in the library.
