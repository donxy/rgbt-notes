### Scope and Definitions

This theme includes mechanisms in fusion that are driven by quality, reliability, conflict, or scene-condition signals and actually reduce the contribution of a modality, including continuous weight attenuation, evidence discounting, rerouting of expert routing, and shutoff of an entire pathway. The problem settings of the member papers are mostly cases in which, under modality imbalance, sensor failure, or distribution shift, a damaged modality pollutes the fusion result through a coupled pathway (source: compare_fields.csv, the problem field of the member papers).

### Overview within the Corpus

Of the 102 papers, 20 have Yes in the suppression column (source: compare_fields.csv, count of the suppression column, 20/102). According to their evidence fields, the implementation forms of suppression can be summarized as follows:

- **Continuous weight attenuation**: the fusion weight is taken directly as a function of a quality / uncertainty quantity — the weight is a negative linear function of uncertainty (zhang2023provable), the RGB injection weight is taken directly as the scalar q (feng2026infranet), or an illumination gate derives the weights of the two pathways to scale the feature maps before proposals (zhou2020improving) (source: compare_fields.csv, the suppression_evidence fields of the above papers).
- **Hard shutoff and exclusion of an entire pathway**: the pathway of a damaged modality is completely cut off — when the entire LiDAR pathway is dropped, 92% of the queries are rerouted to the camera expert, and when the camera is dropped, 100% are rerouted to the LiDAR expert (park2025resilient); when a modality is missing, the entire pathway is excluded with a hard mask of w=0 (zhang2024when); the proposals of the two branches compete by confidence in a top-k manner, and the queries of one branch may be entirely unselected (tian2026modalitydecoupled) (source: compare_fields.csv, the suppression_evidence fields of the above papers).
- **Disagreement / conflict-driven suppression**: inconsistency between the predictions of the two modalities is used as a signal to suppress only the perceived side — the disagreement weight Wd=1−|p1r−p1s| is multiplied only onto the sensed features (zhang2019weakly, zhang2025weakly); when the single-modality predictions of the two pathways have opposite signs, the natural direct effect of thermal infrared is subtracted (kim2024causal); uncertainty plays a structural discounting role in the Dempster combination, and when u→1, the view almost automatically exits (han2021trusted) (source: compare_fields.csv, the suppression_evidence fields of the above papers).
- **Scene-condition prior-driven**: the driving signal is illumination or condition semantics rather than a measurement of the health of the current input — the illumination gate makes w_color approach 0 when illumination is poor (li2019illuminationaware), w_R drops sharply at night (yang2022baanet), and a CLIP condition prompt produces per-channel soft gating weights (chen2025fusion) (source: compare_fields.csv, the suppression_evidence fields of the above papers).
- **Test-time reweighting**: adaptation occurs at the fusion stage — after the cross-modal attention projection is updated at test time, the clean modality receives higher attention, and the self-attention and attended weight of the damaged modality are suppressed (yang2024testtime) (source: compare_fields.csv, the suppression_evidence of yang2024testtime).
- **Upper limit on shutoff for soft gating**: the weight structures of multiple members structurally cannot reach zero — the Sigmoid soft weight of li2024multiscale cannot reach zero and has no cross-modal normalization; w_v + w_t ≡ 1 in hu2025eidet makes the two pathways always enter fusion at the same time, with no abstention pathway; the softmax of chen2025fusion is always positive, with no complete shutoff (source: compare_fields.csv, the limitations fields of the above papers).
- **Asymmetric suppression**: several members suppress only one pathway, and the other pathway is never suppressed — the disagreement weight of zhang2019weakly and zhang2025weakly acts only on the sensed side, and the reference side is never suppressed by disagreement; the soft gating of hou2026leryolo modulates only the aligned RGB features, and the infrared pathway is not modulated (source: compare_fields.csv, the suppression_evidence fields of the above papers).

### Representative Work

(Source of this section: compare_fields.csv, the problem and fusion_strategy fields of each paper)

- park2025resilient — Addressing the problem that a single sensor failure under intermediate fusion pollutes overall perception, it hard-routes by object query on the concatenated features to one of three experts: LiDAR / camera / fusion.
- feng2026infranet — For negative transfer when RGB is severely degraded, it uses the scalar q to modulate the injection weight of RGB guidance into IR and the IR gain, and borrows RGB to shape the IR representation only when RGB is reliable.
- zhang2023provable — It uses a negative linear function of energy-score uncertainty as the decision-level fusion weight, and the modality with higher uncertainty has its weight suppressed lower.
- tian2026modalitydecoupled — Two independent detectors exchange queries and proposals only at the decoder stage, compete by confidence in a top-k manner to form a shared proposal set, and the entire pipeline has no feature-level fusion.
- yang2024testtime — Addressing the reliability bias of multimodal TTA, it updates only the parameters of the attention fusion layer at test time, so that the damaged modality is reweighted and suppressed at the fusion stage.
- wu2026bridging — A per-channel gate driven by a spectral reliability descriptor mixes between a spectral residual branch and a conservative two-pathway mean, automatically falls back to the mean when consistency is weak, and suppresses the contribution of unreliable cross-modal interaction.

### Open Problems (As Stated in the Papers)

- The gating signal is a scene illumination prior rather than a modality health estimate, and there is no pathway for discovering thermal infrared damage and discarding it; nighttime gating only reduces the magnitude of negative fusion and does not eliminate it (under this setting, it is still inferior to thermal infrared alone as a single modality) (source: the Limitations of li2019illuminationaware).
- Hard routing is all-or-nothing, with no partial attenuation and with granularity stopping at the query; failure is mainly by information removal, with no real sensor failure model (source: the Limitations of park2025resilient).
- When the input is corrupted, pattern discrimination can be flipped and the framework has no protection; there is zero evaluation of a damaged modality, and no synthetic corruption or sensor failure model (source: the Limitations of kim2024causal).
- The evaluation protocol covers only the setting of "exactly one modality damaged", and simultaneous damage of both modalities, missing modality, and mixed shift are all uncovered (source: the Limitations of yang2024testtime).
- The degradation evaluation has only one binary total-loss endpoint, and in particular has not evaluated the case in which a damaged modality still produces high-confidence erroneous proposals; the retention under total loss mainly comes from the structural property of decision-level independence, and is not unique to the mechanism of this paper (source: the Limitations of tian2026modalitydecoupled).
- Differential fusion has no modality isolation capability, pollution in one pathway will be injected into the healthy modality along the complementary channel, and there is no pathway within the mechanism to suppress one pathway to zero (source: the Limitations of zhou2020improving).
- The illumination weights sum to 1 and there is no abstention pathway, the only degree of freedom of the weights is the magnitude of rebalancing near equal weighting, and local damage cannot be expressed (source: the Limitations of hu2025eidet).
