### Scope and Definitions

This theme is formed by the union of the modality-imbalance and thermal-only-inference tags in papers.csv, comprising 14 papers in total, of which 10 are core papers and 4 are appendix papers (source: the member list of this theme's pack and statistics over the list field, 14 papers). "Imbalance" has three different referents among the members: the optimization suppression of the weak modality by the dominant modality in joint training; the systematic reversal of the contributions of the two modalities at inference time with conditions (illumination, degradation); and insufficient single-modality learning in joint training, such that the upper bound of fusion is held back by the quality of single-modality representations. Connected to this is the other end, the form of deployment: paired data may be used in training, while at inference only one stream (in most cases thermal infrared) runs in single-modality mode.

### Overview within the Library

- Imbalance in optimization during training is the common problem setting of the appendix members: multimodal gradients conflict in direction with single-modality gradients, and the dominant modality suppresses the slowly learning modality in both gradient magnitude and direction, so that fusion is even inferior to the best single modality (examples: peng2022balanced, fan2023pmr, wei2024mmpareto) (source: the problem of each of the above papers).
- The corresponding rebalancing means are all training-time plug-ins that do not change the inference structure: online gradient modulation, prototype-level loss rebalancing, corrected Pareto gradient integration, and alternating modality training with KL harmonization; at inference time there is either no mechanism, or concatenation/summation/equal-weight score summation of the base remains (examples: peng2022balanced, fan2023pmr, hua2024reconboost) (source: the fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- Imbalance in contribution at inference time appears in two forms among the core members: modality degradation is uniformly modelled as extreme imbalance and addressed by cross-gating with per-modality quality masks; or imbalance is indexed to day–night illumination and addressed by differential fusion with illumination gating (examples: tian2025learning, zhou2020improving) (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- "Fusion degradation" is another attribution path: certain targets that a single modality can detect are instead missed by multimodality, which is attributed to insufficient single-modality learning in joint training or to the suppression of positive-sample gradients by coupling terms; the countermeasures are single-modality distillation or gradient decoupling during training, removed at inference time (examples: zhao2025rethinking, shao2025representation) (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- Single-modality deployment constitutes the other end of the theme: paired data are used during training and only one arbitrary stream is input at inference; only thermal infrared is used in both training and testing, with knowledge restored by generating pseudo-visible images; or an RGB teacher distils during training and only thermal infrared is used at inference — the common feature of the three forms is that no dual-stream fusion exists at inference time (examples: medeiros2025mixed, li2025pseudo, ranasinghe2026thermaldet) (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of each of the above papers).
- Works on training dynamics generally have no evaluation in the damaged/missing dimensions: many members state in their limitations that evaluation of missing, damaged, and failure cases is zero, and their conclusions apply only to settings in which training is complete and clean and the test distribution is unchanged (examples: peng2022balanced, fan2023pmr, wei2024mmpareto, shao2025representation) (source: the Limitations of each of the above papers).

### Representative Works

- peng2022balanced: rebalances joint training during training with online gradient modulation (OGM-GE); it is the representative work of gradient-level rebalancing and does not change the fusion and inference structure (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of peng2022balanced).
- fan2023pmr: prototype-level modality rebalancing (PMR), validated as a training-time loss-level plug-in on multiple base fusion operators including concatenation, summation, FiLM, and gating (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of fan2023pmr).
- hua2024reconboost: achieves modality harmonization through alternating modality training with KL harmonization regularization, and proves its equivalence to gradient boosting; at inference time, the scores of the learners of the respective modalities are summed with equal weights (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of hua2024reconboost).
- zhao2025rethinking: names and attributes the Fusion Degradation phenomenon — insufficient single-modality learning holds back the upper bound of fusion; it is addressed by single-modality distillation retained only during training together with lightweight illumination-aware fusion (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of zhao2025rethinking).
- medeiros2025mixed: during training, modality indistinguishability is achieved through input-level patch mosaic mixing with gradient reversal; at inference, detection can be performed by inputting only one arbitrary stream, exchanging a training technique for single-modality deployment (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of medeiros2025mixed).
- ranasinghe2026thermaldet: RGB teacher response-level distillation with language supervision yields open-vocabulary thermal detection with zero thermal infrared annotations, with only the thermal infrared single modality at inference time (source: the problem and fusion_strategy of ranasinghe2026thermaldet).

### Open Problems (As Stated in the Papers)

- As the authors themselves acknowledge: after gradient modulation is added, the single-modality performance within the joint model still does not exceed that of the best single-modality model, and there is no lower-bound or control experimental design of the form "fusion is not inferior to discarding one stream"; evaluation of missing, damaged, failure, and domain shift cases is all zero (source: the Limitations of peng2022balanced).
- Rebalancing is not equivalent to discarding one stream: on CG-MNIST, the fusion accuracy after rebalancing remains far below that of the grayscale single modality; its measure of imbalance is self-referential — ρ_t and the prototypes come from the representations of the encoder itself that is being trained, and have not been calibrated against true single-modality accuracy (source: the Limitations of fan2023pmr).
- Modality-agnostic detection has no guarantee of a performance lower bound, the information-theoretic argument relies on the unverified assumption that the noise of the two sensors is similar, and, viewed stream by stream, the RGB side remains inferior to models trained specifically for it (source: the Limitations of medeiros2025mixed).
- The number of Fusion Degradation cases has not been reduced to zero; the lower bound of its illumination weighting weight is 0.3, so regions in which RGB has completely failed are still forcibly retained with at least 30% RGB features, and there is no evidence on whether the method holds when a modality is genuinely damaged at inference time (source: the Limitations of zhao2025rethinking).
- The debiasing structure is asymmetric: only one direct-effect chain of thermal infrared is pruned, and the two single-modality heads are presupposed to be trustworthy; when the input is corrupted, the pattern discrimination can be reversed, and the framework has no protection and no discussion (source: the Limitations of kim2024causal).
- The pseudo-visible image is a deterministic function of the thermal infrared input, not independent evidence, so the two streams cannot cross-validate each other, constituting a single-point-of-failure structure; generation quality is determined by the frozen generative prior, and the detection loss cannot correct generation errors (source: the Limitations of li2025pseudo).
